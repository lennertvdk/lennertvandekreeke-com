Research

Since 2024

Doctorate, University of Zurich

At the Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich, supervised by Milan Scheidegger. I’m looking at what DMT and harmine do to cortisol, prolactin and tryptophan catabolites.

Since 2026

EPIsoDE study, Charité

Psilocybin for treatment-resistant depression. I transcribe and analyse recordings of the study visits.

2023

Research assistant, Charité and Zurich

A year working on three clinical studies: EPIsoDE, PRISM (neurofeedback for PTSD) and a 5-MeO-DMT study on embodiment and pain perception.

Organizing

Since 2025

Psychedelic Student Network Germany

I started it with two other students in 2025. It now has over 250 members in 13 cities. I look after strategy, money, partnerships and IT, and I rebuilt the website myself. On Instagram as @psng.info.

Since 2026

Berlin Psychedelic Science Association

PSNG’s Berlin group. Talks, community evenings, the website. On Instagram as @bpsa.berlin.

Ongoing

PSNG Lectures

A lecture series we record and put online. I edit the videos and make the thumbnails. So far: Prateep Beed, Eric Lonergan and Torsten Passie. Watch them on YouTube.

Aug 2026

Ein Abend rund um Psychedelika

Our first in-person PSNG × BPSA evening, at the Molecule office in Berlin. A talk, a workshop, an integration talk and a sound meditation. 31 people came and gave it 9/10 on average.

Since 2024

Awareness Lectures on Psychedelics in Switzerland. I coordinated the participants for the 2025 edition and ran the whole 2026 one: team, programme, marketing and budget. Now I’m mostly advising.

Student group at the University of Zurich and ETH. We ran a lecture series that drew 50 to 100 people and a Swiss student conference.

The student council’s psychiatry group. Talks, events, and the fundraising to pay for them.
